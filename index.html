About

Kithsiri Athulathmudali, also known as Keith, is a seasoned professional with a diverse background spanning entrepreneurship, authorship, and international diplomacy. With a keen interest in aviation, he holds a pilot's license and has taken on significant leadership roles across various industries.

His career, which includes extensive time in the United States and serving as the Ambassador-designate of Sri Lanka to the State of Qatar, is a testament to his dedication to service and creating long-term value.
